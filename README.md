## Torvalds LIANG的2D游戏引擎 ##
一个简单的2D游戏引擎，没有很优雅地设计架构，以此换取简单性，所以尤其适合零基础人群。
A beginner-friendly 2D game engine, deliberately trading architectural purity for simplicity.

FAQ  
**Q: 作者是Torvalds吗**  
不是，作者是Torvalds的铁粉，所以他的英文名叫Torvalds，并且他会开源自己的所有项目。  
**Q: 为什么全是屎山代码**  
因为绝大多数内容都是AI写的，所以改引擎也建议AI改。  

**Q: Is the author really Torvalds?**  
No. But he is a die-hard fan of Linus Torvalds, so Torvalds is his English name, and he shows you the code.  
**Q: Why is the codebase such a mountain of shit?**  
Because the vast majority of it was written by AI — so when it comes to modifying the engine, I recommend letting AI do that too.
